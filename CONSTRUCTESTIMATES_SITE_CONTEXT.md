## 1. Executive Summary & Website Architecture

### Company Overview
**Construct Estimates** is a premier professional construction cost estimating, material takeoff, bid management, and quantity surveying firm operating across the **United States** and **Australia**. Certified by international bodies such as the **American Association of Cost Engineers (AACE)** and the **Australian Institute of Quantity Surveyors (AIQS)**, the company serves general contractors, subcontractors, home builders, commercial developers, architects, engineers, lenders, and insurance adjusters.

### Core Value Proposition & Service Highlights
- **Slogan & Philosophy:** *"Bid More, Win More"* — empowering contractors to bid competitively and increase project win rates by up to 95%.
- **Speed & Turnaround:** Standard project turnaround of **24 to 48 hours** for standard residential and commercial scopes (4-5 days for massive industrial/complex builds).
- **Cost Savings:** Clients save up to **50% in overhead** compared to maintaining an in-house estimating department.
- **Introductory Offers:** 30% to 50% discount on initial orders / first estimates.
- **Accuracy Guarantee:** Zip-code specific pricing via **RSMeans** and the **Craftsman National Construction Estimator Database Suite**.
- **Deliverables:** CSI MasterFormat-coded Excel spreadsheets, color-coded PDF plan markups (Bluebeam / PlanSwift), material cut lists, labor hour estimates, equipment cost schedules, and bid summaries.
- **Revisions & Policies:** Unlimited minor revisions and amendments without extra cost; 95% quote acceptance rate; risk-free refund policy.

### Estimating Software Stack
- **Takeoff & Measurement:** PlanSwift, Bluebeam Revu, Planswift Pro.
- **MEP Estimating:** FastPIPE, FastDUCT, AccuBid, Trimble.
- **Cost Databases:** RSMeans (zip code adjusted), Craftsman National Estimator Suite, proprietary historical price indices.

## 6. Synthesis & Strategic Insights for Development

### Key Takeaways for Building / Redesigning the Platform:
1. **Clear Trust Signals:** Accreditation badges (AACE, AIQS), reviews (Google, Angi, Porch, Thumbtack, NBC), and sample deliverables are critical to the company's conversion rate.

3. **Dual Market Operations (US & AU):** Phone numbers, addresses, and estimating standards must seamlessly cater to both United States (CSI MasterFormat, Imperial, RSMeans) and Australian (AIQS, Metric, AS/NZS codes) clients.
4. **Simple Plan Upload Funnel:** The friction in getting an estimate is minimized by providing immediate drag-and-drop plan uploads with optional scope selection.
